Agent Desk Tape Archive privacy policy

Agent Desk Tape Archive is a personal utility for backing up the account owner's completed market-data recordings.

Data and purpose

The uploader reads completed recording files from the owner's server, compresses them, and stores the archives in the owner's chosen Google Drive folder. File names, sizes, timestamps, checksums, and Drive file identifiers support upload verification and prevent duplicate uploads.

Google Drive access

The utility uses Google Drive authorization to create archive files, inspect uploaded files, and verify their integrity. Its configured destination is the owner's archive folder. OAuth credentials are stored in an encrypted configuration on the owner's server with operating-system access controls.

Storage and access

Original recordings remain on the owner's server. Compressed backups are delivered to Google Drive under the account owner's control. Access to those backups follows that account's Drive permissions. Temporary compressed copies are removed after verification, and local upload receipts record successful backups.

Retention and choices

The account owner controls retention of recordings and uploaded backups. The owner can stop the scheduled uploader, remove backups from Drive, and revoke the app's authorization through Google Account settings.

Google API data

Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google account data is used to provide the requested backup and verification functions.

Contact

For questions about this utility or data requests, open an issue in the app information repository.
